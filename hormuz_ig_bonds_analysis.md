# Investment-Grade Corporate Bonds: US Midstream & Refiners — Hormuz Disruption Thesis

**Date:** March 24, 2026

---

## Macro Context: The 2026 Strait of Hormuz Crisis

Following joint US-Israel military strikes on Iran on February 28, 2026 (including the killing of Supreme Leader Khamenei), Iran's IRGC effectively halted commercial shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. Insurance withdrawal has done the work that physical blockade has not — the strait is effectively closed.

**Key market data (as of mid-March 2026):**
- Brent crude peaked at ~$126/bbl, currently trading ~$109-113
- WTI crude at ~$95-98/bbl (up ~41%)
- Brent-WTI spread has blown out to $12-15/bbl (vs. historical ~$3-5)
- Diesel crack spread: ~$64/bbl (78% of the Oct 2022 all-time record of $83)
- 3-2-1 crack spread: ~$40/bbl
- ~17.5 mb/d of flows disrupted (13.4 mb/d crude + 4.1 mb/d refined products)
- Only 4-7 mb/d of bypass pipeline capacity available (20-35% of pre-disruption flows)

**Investment thesis:** US midstream operators and refiners are direct beneficiaries. Midstream benefits from higher throughput as US production ramps to fill the global gap. Refiners benefit from the Brent-WTI spread explosion — they buy cheap WTI and sell products priced off expensive Brent. Both should see credit improvement, potential spread tightening, and possible upgrades.

## Ranking by Hormuz Torque (Best to Worst)

1. **MPC 5.15% Mar 2030** — Largest US refiner, BBB rated, already low leverage, maximum crack spread exposure. The best risk/reward bond in this universe. At sustained $40+ cracks, Debt/EBITDA drops below 1x and upgrade becomes near-certain.

2. **VLO 4.00% 2029** — Most diesel-levered independent refiner, BBB rated. At $64 diesel cracks, generates extraordinary cash flow. Slight edge to MPC on total throughput capacity.

3. **ET 3.75% May 2030** — Highest current leverage (4.6x) among the group = widest current spread = most room to compress. Benefits from volume growth across largest US pipeline system. Higher risk but higher potential return.

4. **MPLX 4.00% Mar 2028** — Solid midstream play with new pipeline capacity coming online H2 2026. Shorter duration limits total return but reduces risk. Acquisition-driven leverage bump should reverse.

5. **PSX 4.95% Dec 2027** — Diversified but already BBB+/A3. Short duration, limited torque. Best as a defensive position.

6. **EPD 4.60% 2031** — Best credit quality (A-) but that means the least room for improvement. A "sleep well at night" hold, not a torque play.

## Key Risks

- **Hormuz reopening:** Rapid de-escalation would collapse crack spreads and the Brent-WTI differential, removing the torque thesis. Refiner bonds (MPC, VLO) would give back any spread tightening; midstream bonds (ET, MPLX, EPD) would be less affected due to fee-based revenue.
- **Demand destruction:** $100+ oil could trigger recession, reducing refined product demand and refiner margins.
- **SPR release:** The US has signaled potential strategic reserve releases, which would narrow the Brent-WTI spread.
- **Refiner maintenance:** Unplanned outages reduce throughput and margin capture.
- **Credit spread widening:** A broader risk-off event could overwhelm issuer-specific improvements.
- **Energy Transfer leverage:** At 4.6x, ET has less cushion if volumes disappoint or capex overruns occur.
